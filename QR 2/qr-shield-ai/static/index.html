      How It Works

      1. Your browser decodes the QR image with jsQR. 2. The link is analyzed by a Python rule engine using real URL parsing. 3. A reputation service is queried from the server, or a labelled demo fixture is used. 4. You get a report listing every factor and its weight. 5. A warning screen stands between you and any external site.

      Honest limits

      The score is a transparent sum of rules, not a probability of attack. GREEN means no known threat was found by the available checks. It never means safe. Redirect tracing is simulated unless live tracing is enabled on the server.

      Privacy

      The link you analyze is sent to your own Flask server. If a VirusTotal key is configured, only scheme, host and path (no query, fragment or credentials) are sent to VirusTotal. API keys stay on the server.
